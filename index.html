Research

I work on physical AI and robot learning, with a focus on safety: how robots can plan and act from what they see while respecting hard physical limits. My work spans constrained world-model planning, visual navigation, and state estimation for aerial robots. Representative papers are highlighted.

Safe Planning for Contact-Rich Robotic Manipulation
H. T. Bagci, J. Bi, F. E. Yalcindag, X. Zhou, E. Koyuncu, Z. Cao
Under review at ICLR, 2027

A layer that projects each planned action onto the set the robot's controller can execute within a force limit, in closed form and with no online optimization. On peg insertion, gear meshing, and nut threading it keeps contact forces under the cap at every step, with 98.1% safe success on peg insertion at 5 N.

CONCORD: Constraint-Aware Decision Support Framework for Tactical Air COA Generation and Ranking
H. T. Bagci, E. Koyuncu, N. K. Ure
AIAA SciTech Forum, 2027 (to appear)

Generates candidate air-mission plans, scores them under stochastic threats with Monte Carlo simulation, and ranks them against explicit hard and soft constraints, with a readable rationale for each choice.

TANGO-VIO: Triangulation-Aware Navigation with Guaranteed Feature-Observability for Visual-Inertial Odometry
A. Şanlan, E. C. Altunkaya, H. T. Bagci, E. Koyuncu, İ. Özkol
arXiv, 2026 (under review at IEEE T-RO)
arXiv/PDF

A control barrier function on a log-determinant triangulation metric nudges the drone's velocity just enough to keep visual features observable, so VIO stays well-conditioned under low-parallax motion. Validated in simulation and real flights.

Particle Filter-Based Localization Using Visual Feature Synchronization in GNSS-Denied Navigation
A. Şanlan, H. T. Bagci, E. Koyuncu
ICNS, 2025  Best of Session Award
IEEE/DOI

A CycleGAN maps winter drone imagery into the summer look of satellite maps, and a marginalized particle filter fuses the resulting feature matches with inertial navigation to localize a UAV without GNSS. Tested in real flights.

Projects

Visual Navigation from Time-to-Transit
Boston University Robotics Lab, 2025–present

Estimating depth from time-to-transit cues in monocular optical flow, then learning a diffusion navigation policy on top of it from expert demonstrations, with closed-form action constraints for safety. Shown: a Jackal running the policy in Isaac Sim.
